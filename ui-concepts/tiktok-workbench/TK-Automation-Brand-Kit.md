# TK Automation

## Brand Personality
Precise / Controlled / Efficient / Professional

## Primary
`#2856D8` — Cobalt Blue. Used for focus, navigation, primary actions and measured direction.

## Accent
`#168A57` — Signal Green. Used only for healthy, ready and completed states.

## Supporting Colors
- Warning Amber `#C47A12`
- Danger Red `#C2413B`
- Slate `#1F2937`

## Background
`#111827` — Charcoal control-room canvas.

## Surface
`#1F2937` dark surface / `#F3F5F7` light workspace surface.

## Typography
Primary: Fira Sans, with Microsoft YaHei UI fallback for Chinese. Data, IDs and command-like values: Fira Code. Use sentence case, tight hierarchy, and tabular numerals.

## Iconography
Thin-line operational icons with consistent 1.5–2px stroke: control, filter, sync, pause, alert, export, table, status. Use Lucide-style SVG icons; no emoji, no decorative sparkles.

## Visual Motif
Measured operator rail: a rectangular control frame, a single directional path, and a node indicating a monitored state. The mark combines a restrained TK monogram with a precise route.

## Logo
TK monogram inside a control frame. The K diagonal extends into a cobalt-blue route ending in a circular node/arrow. It scales as a square app icon, horizontal wordmark, status badge and UI mark.

## Texture
Quiet charcoal, steel and mist surfaces; subtle paper grain and construction lines. Use contrast and alignment as the premium signal, not glow.

## Image Style
Art-directed operator environments: dark control-room desks, campaign plans, monitors, measured diagrams and restrained blue/green task signals. No generic people or stock-office scenes.

## Avoid
Cyberpunk neon, purple-blue AI gradients, excessive glow, glassmorphism, generic SaaS cards, random symbols, overcomplicated marks, fake tiny copy, rainbow palettes and platform-trademark imitation.

## Suggested Tagline
**Every campaign under control.**
